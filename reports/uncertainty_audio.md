# Uncertainty and open-set flags (audio)

Each window gets one of four states: **drone**, **no drone**, **unknown drone** (scored drone but unlike every training drone) or **low confidence** (score near the threshold, or input unlike anything in training). All cut-offs come from validation data and were fixed before testing: review band = 10% of validation windows nearest the threshold; unfamiliar = beyond the 95% quantile of validation distance (Mahalanobis on 64 PCA components). Generated by `scripts/eval_uncertainty.py`.

## Do the flags catch the errors?

Errors are counted at the frozen 1%-FPR threshold. AURC = area under the risk-coverage curve (lower is better; `random` is the no-information reference).

| tier | error rate | flagged | error rate, unflagged | share of errors flagged | unfamiliar | AURC margin | AURC spread | AURC distance | AURC random |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tier 1 (indoor held-out) | 0.020 | 0.127 | 0.005 | 0.800 | 0.058 | 0.003 | 0.014 | 0.021 | 0.017 |
| tier 3 (indoor -> outdoor) | 0.244 | 0.418 | 0.218 | 0.482 | 0.017 | 0.179 | 0.300 | 0.315 | 0.244 |
| tier 4 (+ outdoor training) | 0.267 | 0.116 | 0.245 | 0.188 | 0.019 | 0.188 | 0.315 | 0.349 | 0.273 |

## Open set: unseen drone model

Familiarity fitted on Bebop drone windows from 70% of Bebop/background recordings, calibrated on 15%, tested on the other 15% (known drone) and on every Mambo window (unknown drone).

- Distance separates Mambo from held-out Bebop at AUROC **0.808**.
- Flagged as unlike any training drone: Bebop 15.5% (110 windows), Mambo 35.6% (666 windows).

| windows | no drone | drone | unknown drone | low confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bebop | 4.5% | 76.4% | 4.5% | 14.5% |
| mambo | 8.7% | 11.0% | 1.4% | 79.0% |

## What this shows

- **The margin is the useful signal.** Ranking by distance from the threshold beats random on every tier. On tier 1, the 13% of windows sent to review hold 80% of the errors.
- **The familiarity flag does not detect the move outdoors.** Only 1.7% of Svanström windows are flagged unfamiliar to the indoor-only model, and ranking by distance is no better than random. The outdoor shift apparently lies outside the 64 main directions of the training embeddings. A residual-based distance might catch it, but choosing it now would mean tuning on the test set, so it is left for a new test set.
- **Ensemble spread adds nothing** over the margin here: the head is strongly regularised, so bootstrap members agree.
- **Open set works in ranking, less in calibration.** Mambo is farther from known drones than held-out Bebop (AUROC above), but the flag rate on held-out Bebop is above the nominal 5% because the calibration split holds few recordings. Mambo rarely scores above the drone threshold, so most Mambo windows end as low confidence, not unknown drone.

## Caveats

- Only two drone models exist in the indoor data, so the open-set test is a single pair (Bebop known, Mambo unknown).
- The 'what this shows' notes were written after seeing these results; the cut-offs themselves were not changed.
- The tier-4 detector is saved to `data/models/detector.joblib`; its states are what `detect.py` and the dashboard show.
