# Audio models trained from scratch: CNN vs CNN+GRU vs transformer

The proposal's audio comparison. All three see the same cached 1 s log-mel (128 x 63), with the clip's mean level removed, per-band standardisation from train, SpecAugment, AdamW with a one-cycle schedule and 20 fixed epochs. Threshold at 1% FPR on validation negatives, unchanged on test. Generated by `scripts/train_deep.py`.

| model | parameters |
|---|---|
| cnn | 210,833 |
| cnn+gru | 269,905 |
| transformer | 317,377 |

- tier 1: held-out recordings, same indoor corpus
- tier 2: train Bebop, test Mambo; threshold from 20% of training recordings held out (one fit, not 5 folds)
- tier 3: train indoor, test outdoor Svanström
- tier 4: tier 3 plus ITU ARIS outdoor drones in training, source-balanced

Accuracy and F1 depend on the test set's drone share (12-33% here, far above real life), so read PR-AUC and recall at the fixed false-alarm rate first.
