# RF cross-dataset check: DroneRF forest on Tampere recordings

Saved DroneRF forest, threshold 0.262 (1% FPR on DroneRF validation), applied unchanged to the Tampere anechoic-chamber recordings (Zenodo 4264467): new receiver, new session, drones never trained on. Each 2.44 GHz / 120 MS/s file is mixed to DroneRF's two 40 MHz bands and decimated to 40 MS/s; the real part stands in for DroneRF's single channel (an assumption). Generated by `scripts/eval_rf_tampere.py`.

- windows: 480 of 25 ms
- recall on windows with a visible transmission: **1.000** (472 windows)
- windows at the noise floor in both bands (< floor + 7 dB): 8; called drone: **1.000**, median score 0.902

- **pure white noise** through the same conversion (40 windows, no drone at all): called drone **1.000**, median score 0.876

**Verdict: the DroneRF forest does not detect drones.** It calls 100% of pure-noise windows "drone", so its rule is "not the DroneRF background recording". The 100% recall above says nothing about detection, and the same shortcut explains the near-perfect DroneRF scores and the quiet-window AUC of 1.000 in `rf_detection.md`. A usable RF detector needs training negatives from more than one session and receiver.

No drone-off recordings exist in this corpus, so no false-alarm rate is measured. Quiet windows are the closest stand-in: a detector that reads drone signals should call them "no drone".
