# RF detector v2: trained to require a transmission

v1 calls pure noise "drone" (`rf_tampere.md`). **v2** keeps v1's features and forest and changes the data, decided before any v2 result was seen: DroneRF drone windows at the noise floor are no longer labelled drone; synthetic receiver noise (white, coloured) through both the DroneRF and the Tampere pipelines is added as negatives; Tampere drones from a second receiver are added as positives, split by drone. Pink noise is a held-out family. Threshold at 1% FPR on validation negatives. Generated by `scripts/train_rf_v2.py`.

**v2b** was added after v2's results were seen: v2 misses DroneRF's Phantom when it is held out, because its video link fills the 40 MHz band flat and level-free shape cannot tell that from a raised noise floor. v2b adds one feature, the level difference between the two bands, and every noise window gets a random band-to-band gain mismatch (+-6 dB) so that feature cannot mean "not a lab receiver". Both variants are trained on this same data, so v2's rows here differ slightly from its first run. The deployment rule (no worse on noise, better on unseen drones) was written before v2b ran. **Deployed: v2.**

Tampere drones in each split: DJI_inspire_2 (train), DJI_matrice_100 (val), DJI_matrice_210 (test), DJI_mavic_mini (val), DJI_mavic_pro (train), DJI_phantom_4 (train), DJI_phantom_4_pro_plus (train), Parrot_disco (train), Parrot_mambo (train), Yuneec_typhoon_h (val). Every Tampere drone is also tested by leaving it out of training.

## What this still cannot show

- Real non-drone emitters (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, microwave ovens) from anywhere but DroneRF's lab. DroneRF background is the only real negative; the datasets with more (DroneDetect, CardRF, Glüge et al. on Kaggle) need a login. Until then, a false-alarm rate on real-world 2.4 GHz traffic is unmeasured.
- Tampere is an anechoic chamber, a far cleaner scene than the field.
- v2b's band-difference feature would also fire on a strong non-drone transmitter in one half of the band; that is exactly the untested case above.
