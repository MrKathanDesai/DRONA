# Live alarm rule: calibration

The live page calls "drone" after *k* drone windows in a row and holds the call for 3 windows. *k* is chosen on tier-4 validation recordings: the fewest false-alarm recordings while catching at least 90% of the drone recordings the most sensitive *k* catches. **Chosen: k = 1.** Generated by `scripts/calibrate_live.py`.

## validation (56 drone, 292 background recordings)

| k | drone recordings alarmed | background recordings alarmed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (chosen) | 0.93 | 0.014 |
| 2 | 0.61 | 0.000 |
| 3 | 0.55 | 0.000 |
| 4 | 0.48 | 0.000 |

## test (outdoor Svanström) (30 drone, 60 background recordings)

| k | drone recordings alarmed | background recordings alarmed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (chosen) | 0.57 | 0.017 |
| 2 | 0.27 | 0.000 |
| 3 | 0.10 | 0.000 |
| 4 | 0.10 | 0.000 |

Validation recordings are indoor DroneAudioDataset and ITU ARIS; the test set is outdoor, where window-level recall is known to be weak (`outdoor_audio.md`).
