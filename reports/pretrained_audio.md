# Pretrained audio detector

Frozen PANNs MobileNetV1 (AudioSet, 4.2 M params, `MobileNetV1_mAP=0.389.pth`) turns each RMS-normalised 1 s clip into a 1024-d embedding; only the sklearn head is trained. Threshold fixed on validation negatives at 1% FPR, applied unchanged to test. CIs resample whole recordings. Generated by `scripts/train_pretrained.py`.

The embedding layer (pooled conv output) and the head's C were chosen from a small grid while looking at these tiers, so the pretrained rows are mildly optimistic; the baseline rows were not tuned this way.

- tier 1: held-out recordings, same indoor corpus
- tier 2: train Bebop, test Mambo (unseen drone model)
- tier 3: train indoor corpus, test outdoor Svanström corpus (different place, microphone and drones; 30 recordings per class)
