# Music masking: detecting a drone under loud music

Indoor drone recordings from the tier-4 validation split (35; the detector's weights never saw them, its threshold did; the tier-1 test split is tier-4 training data) mixed with held-out GTZAN music (60 excerpts) at falling SNR (drone power over music power; -10 dB = music ten times stronger). Negatives: music alone, 60 background recordings, and background + music. 1 s windows, each detector at its own threshold (1% false alarms on its validation negatives). Generated by `scripts/eval_masking.py`; figure `figures/12_masking.png`.

- **stationary front-end**: keeps sound that stays at one frequency for seconds (rotor tones), drops what changes (music), then runs the unchanged detector (`src/dronedet/features/unmask.py`).
- **music-aware detector**: the tier-4 detector rebuilt with drone + music mixtures (-10 to +10 dB) and music alone in training; train/val music never overlaps the test music.

## Reading the results

- **The stationary front-end does not work on real recordings.** It removes enough of a real drone (rotor speed is not perfectly steady) that clean recall collapses, and the steady parts of music (sustained bass and pads) survive it and look drone-like, so music alone raises false alarms. The synthetic test in `tests/test_unmask.py` (a perfectly steady rotor) overstates what it can do. A detector trained on front-end output might recover some of this; that is untested.
- **Training with music works.** The music-aware detector keeps clean recall close to the original and catches over twice as many drone windows at 0 and -5 dB; music alone still raises no alarms. The cost is more false alarms on background noise mixed with music. It is an experiment and is not used on the live page.
- Below -5 dB (music more than three times stronger than the drone) nothing here works.
